Bash Club is a Fighting Game Community located in Kent, England.
We hold weekly casuals and monthly tournaments around the county.

Find Out More

Bouts and Banter

Bash Club is all about having a laugh while trying our hand at fighting games. Focusing maingly on the Street Fighter series, we meet up regularly offline around Kent.

Casuals are held every Monday at The Penny Theatre in Canterbury, as part of their Retro Games night. Tournaments are usually hosted on the first Sunday of every month at Noobz Gaming Lounge in Margate, and come with cash prizes for top 3. Check our Facebook below for exact dates.
